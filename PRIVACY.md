# Articleall Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 27, 2026**

Articleall does not collect, transmit, sell, or share user data. It has no
analytics, advertising, telemetry, account system, or remote server.

## What Articleall accesses

Articleall locally reads top-level navigation URLs in order to determine
whether a supported article can be rewritten to its full-page URL. URL
rewriting happens inside the browser. Navigation URLs are not sent to the
maintainer or to any third party.

The extension only acts on the supported domains listed in its manifest. It
does not read page content, form inputs, browsing history, or authentication
credentials.

## Storage

The enabled state and per-site preferences are stored using the browser's
extension storage. The extension prefers browser sync storage and falls back to
local extension storage when sync storage is unavailable. These settings remain
under the browser's local or sync-storage controls; Articleall does not operate
its own database or transmit them to a service.

## Changes and contact

This policy may be updated with a new effective date. For questions about this
policy or the extension, open an issue in the Articleall repository.
